EduTutor BD – Bengali AI Tutor

Full-stack Bengali AI tutoring platform for SSC students using RAG over NCTB textbooks (2,768 chunks in ChromaDB) and Bloom's Taxonomy-based adaptive questioning. LLaMA 3.3 70B via Groq generates and evaluates questions with Bengali feedback in under 2 seconds. Built with Python, FastAPI, Gemini Embeddings, PHP and MySQL.

Beyond the Menu – RFID Ordering & Delivery Robot

Restaurant ordering system where diners order by tapping RFID cards, with no app, touchscreen or waiter needed. Two ESP32 terminals and an ESP32 delivery car talk over MQTT to a Python/Tkinter kitchen dashboard with live order status, presence detection and a kitchen watchdog.
