2026 Achievement

EduTutor BD reaches the Final Round of The Infinity AI BuildFest 2026

CloudCamp Bangladesh × BRAC University

My Bengali AI tutor, EduTutor BD, was selected for the final round of this national AI build competition.

EduTutor BD started with a simple question: what if every SSC student in Bangladesh had a patient tutor who spoke their language? It reads NCTB textbooks through a retrieval pipeline, asks questions that climb Bloom's Taxonomy, and replies in Bengali in under two seconds. Reaching the final round showed me the idea mattered beyond the classroom.
